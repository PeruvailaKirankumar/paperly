Understanding Bloom's Taxonomy

Bloom's Taxonomy is a hierarchical framework for categorizing educational learning objectives into levels of complexity and specificity. Our AI generator uses this framework to create well-balanced question papers that comprehensively test different cognitive skills.

Remember

Recall facts and basic concepts

  • List, define, identify
  • Name, recall, recognize
  • Who, what, when, where

Understand

Explain ideas or concepts

  • Describe, explain
  • Discuss, summarize
  • Compare, contrast

Apply

Use information in new situations

  • Solve, demonstrate
  • Implement, use
  • Execute, carry out

Analyze

Draw connections among ideas

  • Differentiate, organize
  • Attribute, deconstruct
  • Compare, examine

Evaluate

Justify a stand or decision

  • Judge, critique
  • Assess, validate
  • Rate, prioritize

Create

Produce new or original work

  • Design, construct
  • Develop, produce
  • Formulate, devise
